Important notes

Keep portions small and follow your baby's tolerance.

  • Keep the same fruit for 4–5 consecutive days before changing.
  • Use the same vegetable at lunch and dinner for 4–5 consecutive days before changing.
  • Breakfast uses cooked rava, ragi or soft idli + the day's fruit, rather than another rice meal.
  • Lunch and dinner are soft cooked rice + one vegetable + moong dal.
  • Banana, papaya and avocado can be served ripe and mashed. Steam apple and pear initially.
  • No mango or other seasonal fruit is included in this plan.
  • Continue breast milk/formula according to the baby's usual feeding pattern.
  • Do not force-feed. Start with small quantities and increase gradually according to tolerance.
